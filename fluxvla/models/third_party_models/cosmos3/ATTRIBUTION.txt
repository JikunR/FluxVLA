Cosmos3 / cosmos-framework
==========================

Source: https://github.com/nvidia/cosmos-framework

Only the minimal set of files required by Cosmos3NanoVLA are included (69 source
files, selected via BFS from the VLA entry-point imports). All internal imports
have been rewritten from `cosmos_framework.*` to
`fluxvla.models.third_party_models.cosmos3.*`.

Included modules:
  data/vfm/sequence_packing.py, utils.py
  model/attention/           — PackedAttention, flash2/3/natten backends
  model/vfm/mot/             — Cosmos3VFMNetwork, MoTDecoderLayer, unified_mot
  model/vfm/tokenizers/      — Wan2pt2VAEInterface, VideoTokenizerInterface
  model/vfm/utils/           — GenerationDataClean/Noised
  model/vfm/algorithm/loss/  — flow_matching, time_weight
  model/vfm/diffusion/       — RectifiedFlow, UniPCSampler
  model/vfm/vlm/             — Qwen3VL, Nemotron3DenseVL configs
  utils/                     — log, distributed, flags, easy_io, lazy_config, misc
